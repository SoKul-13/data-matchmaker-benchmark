\begin{figure}[t]\centering\includegraphics[width=\columnwidth]{fig06_anchor_operators.pdf}\caption{What the judge rewards. The lexical rule is generous to superset and shuffled lists and severe on typos and medium near misses; these are the cases human labels must settle.}\label{fig:fig06_anchor_operators}\end{figure}
