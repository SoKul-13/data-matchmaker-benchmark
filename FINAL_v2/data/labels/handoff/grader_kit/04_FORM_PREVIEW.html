How to grade

1. Grade the system's FINAL answer (yellow on the card) against the reference answer (green). Not the reasoning, not the writing. 2. Rating: 7 = I would accept it as the reference answer, 4 = half of it is right, 1 = nothing is right. Between two neighbouring ratings, choose the lower. Yes/no answers are only 7 or 1. 3. Hedged = Yes when the final answer offers more than one candidate, a range where one value was asked, or will not commit. 4. Do not look anything up. If you doubt the reference, grade against it anyway and leave a note.
