LLM-era papers (Peeters–Bizer 2023–25, Jellyfish, ComEM, AnyMatch, Unicorn, Steiner–Bizer 2026) reuse this core; the only new domains are OpenSanctions Pairs (people/orgs) and EMBer (multimodal).
